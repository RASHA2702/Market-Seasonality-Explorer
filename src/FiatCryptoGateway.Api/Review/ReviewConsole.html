Held payments

Paid, and waiting for your decision, longest first. Approving lets the payment settle to the merchant. Rejecting holds the money until you record the report you filed.

Wallets to review

Merchants' new settlement wallets whose screening found risk or could not finish. A wallet on a sanctions list can only be rejected.

Frozen payments

Money from rejected payments, frozen and reported. Release it for refund only when that is allowed.

Reconciliation

Differences between providers' daily reports and the books, from the latest run of each day. "Not in our books" usually means a customer paid but the order did not move.

Failed notifications

Merchant notifications the gateway gave up on in the last 7 days. Sending again tries once more now; it may arrive after newer notifications, so merchants should go by its sequence number.
